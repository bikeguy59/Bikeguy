Welcome! This is the first post on my new blog.

## Overview	
Many years ago, shortly after graduating from college, so about 1983, I wrote a BASIC program called BikeDRAW. I developed the program to draw a traditional bicycle frame based on some basic inputs used by frame builders.

## Evidence
I lost the files to this project a long time ago, but in talking with my son, he was able to track down the supplements to Bicycling magazine that
 included a few writeups about the program, and a break in to my apartment at the time.
